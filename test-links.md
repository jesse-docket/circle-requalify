# Clara - Re-Qualification — test links

Base: `https://jesse-docket.github.io/circle-requalify/`

**Start at the form** — that is the whole point of this build:
https://jesse-docket.github.io/circle-requalify/plus-sales/

Three shortcut buttons under the form fill it instantly. Submit and you land on the page where
Clara lives, already talking.

**Between runs, press ↺ Reset session** (bottom-left of Clara's page). The widget keeps a visitor
id in browser storage, so without a reset the next link reopens the previous person's
conversation. The button clears it before the widget boots. You can also append **`&reset=1`** to
any link below to start that run clean.

## Extras

- **No submission attached** (Clara runs open discovery): https://jesse-docket.github.io/circle-requalify/connect-with-sales/
- **Panel open but silent until clicked:** add `&autostart=0`
- **Force a brand-new visitor:** add `&reset=1`
- **Any other combination:** the link builder in the footer of the Clara page, in plain language.

## What to check

1. Clara opens by herself, within a few seconds, no click.
2. Her first line after the greeting is the visitor's own situation, said back to them — not a
   generic welcome and not a question she already has the answer to.
3. Nothing anywhere says they did not qualify, are too small, or missed a threshold.
4. On link 1 and 3 she asks for the meeting and the **Book a Meeting** button appears.
5. On link 2 she gives the personalized trial link and walks checkout one step per message.
6. Ask her "how do you know all this?" — she should say it came through on the form and move on,
   never mentioning parameters, context or tracking.
7. Ask for a discount — there isn't one, and she shouldn't invent one.
